Muaaz Iqbal

Muaaz Iqbal is a developer, entrepreneur, educator, writer and trader based in Kasur, Punjab, Pakistan. He builds digital products on his own — Android apps, web platforms, Telegram bots and streaming systems — under the MiTV Network, Muslim Islam and MI NEXUS brands.

What he builds

MINEXUS TV is his streaming web app for movies, web series, live TV channels and music. Other projects include MI Trade Master (a trading-education Android app), the MI NEXUS signal bot on Telegram, MI AI bots, MICH Digital Shop and a 24/7 YouTube live stream.

Why MINEXUS TV

The goal is simple: a fast, free, clean place to watch Pakistani, Indian and international entertainment, with Islamic content such as the daily Quran ayat feed alongside it.
