About MINEXUS TV

Our story, our mission, and the team building it.

What Is MINEXUS TV?

MINEXUS TV is a free streaming discovery platform focused on Pakistani, Indian, and international cinema, alongside a growing catalog of web series and live television channels. Our goal is straightforward: give people in Pakistan and across South Asia an easy, ad-supported way to discover what to watch next, browse detailed information about movies and shows, keep track of what they've already seen, and catch up on live news and sports — all from one clean, fast interface that works equally well on a phone or a laptop.

We built MINEXUS TV as a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means it installs like a native app on Android and iOS home screens without needing an app store, loads quickly even on slower mobile connections common across Pakistan, and keeps working offline for content you've already interacted with.

Our Mission

Entertainment discovery shouldn't be complicated. Between multiple streaming services, inconsistent regional availability, and cluttered interfaces, finding something worth watching often takes longer than actually watching it. MINEXUS TV exists to fix that specific problem — a single, well-organized home base for browsing movie and series metadata, ratings, and release calendars, built with genuine care for speed and usability rather than as an afterthought.

What Makes Us Different

  • Regional focus: dedicated sections for Pakistani and Indian cinema alongside Hollywood, instead of treating regional content as an afterthought buried in a generic catalog.
  • Live TV built in: news, sports, and entertainment channels from Pakistan, India, the UAE, UK, and USA, organized by country rather than scattered across unrelated categories.
  • A real music section: song search, playlists, and offline downloads, so switching between video and audio content never means leaving the app.
  • Community features: a leaderboard that rewards active users, and a personal watchlist/history that syncs across devices for anyone who creates a free account.
  • Genuinely free to browse: creating an account is optional and only unlocks extras like cross-device sync — nobody is required to sign up or pay just to explore the catalog.
