Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MINEXUS TV ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and app. By using MINEXUS TV, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: if you sign in with Google or email, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from that provider.
  • Usage data: what you watch, search for, add to your watchlist, and like — stored against your account so features like watch history and recommendations work.
  • Device information: basic technical data (browser type, general region) used for things like defaulting the right live-TV country and diagnosing errors.

How We Use Information

  • To operate core features: watch history, watchlist, likes, search history, and the referral/points system.
  • To show relevant content and improve the catalog.
  • To communicate app updates or important notices via in-app notifications.

Third-Party Services

MINEXUS TV uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication and database storage for your account data.
  • TMDB / Cinemeta: movie and series metadata (titles, posters, descriptions) — we don't send them any personal information about you.
  • Google AdSense: may show ads and use cookies/device identifiers to personalize them. See Google's policy on partner sites for details.
  • Third-party streaming embed providers: when you play a title, the video is served by an external embed provider. We don't control their data practices; avoid entering personal information on any page that isn't part of minexustv.vercel.app itself.

Your Choices

  • You can review and delete your watch history, watchlist, and likes at any time from your Account page.
  • You can sign out at any time, which stops new activity from being linked to your account.
  • To request full deletion of your account data, contact us using the details below.

Data Security

We use Firebase's security infrastructure and access-controlled database rules to protect stored data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Children's Privacy

MINEXUS TV is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out via the contact links on our Developer page.
